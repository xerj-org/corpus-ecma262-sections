ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.3 Operations on Objects > 7.3.31 PrivateSet ( _obj_: an Object, _privateName_: a Private Name, _value_: an ECMAScript language value, ): either a normal completion containing ~unused~ or a throw completion [id: sec-privateset]

<emu-clause id="sec-privateset" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        PrivateSet (
          _obj_: an Object,
          _privateName_: a Private Name,
          _value_: an ECMAScript language value,
        ): either a normal completion containing ~unused~ or a throw completion
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _entry_ be PrivateElementFind(_obj_, _privateName_).
        1. If _entry_ is ~empty~, throw a *TypeError* exception.
        1. If _entry_.[[Kind]] is ~method~, throw a *TypeError* exception.
        1. If _entry_.[[Kind]] is ~field~, then
          1. Set _entry_.[[Value]] to _value_.
        1. Else,
          1. Assert: _entry_.[[Kind]] is ~accessor~.
          1. If _entry_.[[Setter]] is *undefined*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. Let _setter_ be _entry_.[[Setter]].
          1. Perform ? Call(_setter_, _obj_, « _value_ »).
        1. Return ~unused~.
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
