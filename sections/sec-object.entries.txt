<emu-clause id="sec-object.entries" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Object.entries ( _obj_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _coerced_ be ? ToObject(_obj_).
          1. Let _entryList_ be ? EnumerableOwnProperties(_coerced_, ~key+value~).
          1. Return CreateArrayFromList(_entryList_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
