ECMA-262 > 29 Memory Model > 29.6 Relations of Candidate Executions > 29.6.6 happens-before [id: sec-happens-before]

<emu-clause id="sec-happens-before">
      <h1>happens-before</h1>
      <p>For a candidate execution _execution_, its <dfn>happens-before</dfn> Relation is the least Relation on Memory events that satisfies the following.</p>

      <ul>
        <li>
          <p>For events _eventA_ and _eventB_, _eventA_ happens-before _eventB_ in _execution_ if any of the following conditions are true.</p>
          <ul>
            <li>_eventA_ is-agent-order-before _eventB_ in _execution_.</li>
            <li>_eventA_ synchronizes-with _eventB_ in _execution_.</li>
            <li>SharedDataBlockEventSet(_execution_) contains both _eventA_ and _eventB_, _eventA_.[[Order]] is ~init~, and _eventA_ and _eventB_ have overlapping memory ranges.</li>
            <li>There is an event _eventC_ such that _eventA_ happens-before _eventC_ and _eventC_ happens-before _eventB_ in _execution_.</li>
          </ul>
        </li>
      </ul>

      <emu-note>
        <p>Because happens-before is a superset of agent-order, a candidate execution is consistent with the single-thread evaluation semantics of ECMAScript.</p>
      </emu-note>
    </emu-clause>
