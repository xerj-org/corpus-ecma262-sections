<emu-clause id="sec-number.max_value">
        <h1>Number.MAX_VALUE</h1>
        <p>The value of `Number.MAX_VALUE` is the largest positive finite Number, which is approximately <emu-eqn>1.7976931348623157 × 10<sup>308</sup></emu-eqn>.</p>
        <p>This property has the attributes { [[Writable]]: *false*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *false* }.</p>
      </emu-clause>
