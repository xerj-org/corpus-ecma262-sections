ECMA-262 > 6 ECMAScript Data Types and Values > 6.1 ECMAScript Language Types > 6.1.6 Numeric Types > 6.1.6.1 The Number Type > 6.1.6.1.8 Number::subtract ( _x_: a Number, _y_: a Number, ): a Number [id: sec-numeric-types-number-subtract]

<emu-clause id="sec-numeric-types-number-subtract" type="numeric method">
          <h1>
            Number::subtract (
              _x_: a Number,
              _y_: a Number,
            ): a Number
          </h1>
          <dl class="header">
            <dt>description</dt>
            <dd>It performs subtraction, producing the difference of its operands; _x_ is the minuend and _y_ is the subtrahend.</dd>
          </dl>
          <emu-alg>
            1. Return Number::add(_x_, Number::unaryMinus(_y_)).
          </emu-alg>
          <emu-note>
            <p>It is always the case that `x - y` produces the same result as `x + (-y)`.</p>
          </emu-note>
        </emu-clause>
