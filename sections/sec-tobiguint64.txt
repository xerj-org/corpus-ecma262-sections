ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.1 Type Conversion > 7.1.18 ToBigUint64 ( _arg_: an ECMAScript language value, ): either a normal completion containing a BigInt or a throw completion [id: sec-tobiguint64]

<emu-clause id="sec-tobiguint64" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        ToBigUint64 (
          _arg_: an ECMAScript language value,
        ): either a normal completion containing a BigInt or a throw completion
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
        <dt>description</dt>
        <dd>It converts _arg_ to one of 2<sup>64</sup> BigInt values in the inclusive interval from *0*<sub>ℤ</sub> to ℤ(2<sup>64</sup> - 1).</dd>
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _int_ be ℝ(? ToBigInt(_arg_)).
        1. Return ℤ(ToFixedSizeInteger(_int_, ~unsigned~, 64)).
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
