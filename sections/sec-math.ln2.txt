        <h1>Math.LN2</h1>
        <p>The Number value for the natural logarithm of 2, which is approximately 0.6931471805599453.</p>
        <p>This property has the attributes { [[Writable]]: *false*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *false* }.</p>
