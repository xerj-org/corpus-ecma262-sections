ECMA-262 > 25 Structured Data > 25.4 The Atomics Object > 25.4.3 Abstract Operations for Atomics > 25.4.3.2 ValidateAtomicAccess ( _taRecord_: a TypedArray With Buffer Witness Record, _requestIndex_: an ECMAScript language value, ): either a normal completion containing a non-negative integer or a throw completion [id: sec-validateatomicaccess]

<emu-clause id="sec-validateatomicaccess" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          ValidateAtomicAccess (
            _taRecord_: a TypedArray With Buffer Witness Record,
            _requestIndex_: an ECMAScript language value,
          ): either a normal completion containing a non-negative integer or a throw completion
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _length_ be TypedArrayLength(_taRecord_).
          1. Let _accessIndex_ be ? ToIndex(_requestIndex_).
          1. Assert: _accessIndex_ ≥ 0.
          1. If _accessIndex_ ≥ _length_, throw a *RangeError* exception.
          1. Let _ta_ be _taRecord_.[[Object]].
          1. Let _elementSize_ be TypedArrayElementSize(_ta_).
          1. Let _offset_ be _ta_.[[ByteOffset]].
          1. Return (_accessIndex_ × _elementSize_) + _offset_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
