        <h1>
          HourFromTime (
            _tv_: a finite time value,
          ): an integer in the inclusive interval from 0 to 23
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
          <dt>description</dt>
          <dd>It returns the hour of the day in which _tv_ falls.</dd>
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Return floor(ℝ(_tv_) / MillisecondsPerHour) modulo HoursPerDay.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
