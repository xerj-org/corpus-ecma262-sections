<emu-clause id="sec-object.getownpropertydescriptor" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptor ( _obj_, _key_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _coerced_ be ? ToObject(_obj_).
          1. Let _propertyKey_ be ? ToPropertyKey(_key_).
          1. Let _propertyDesc_ be ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_coerced_.[[GetOwnProperty]](_propertyKey_)</emu-meta>.
          1. Return FromPropertyDescriptor(_propertyDesc_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
