      <emu-grammar>FormalParameterList : FormalParameterList `,` FormalParameter</emu-grammar>
      <emu-alg>
        1. If IsSimpleParameterList of |FormalParameterList| is *false*, return *false*.
        1. Return IsSimpleParameterList of |FormalParameter|.
      </emu-alg>
