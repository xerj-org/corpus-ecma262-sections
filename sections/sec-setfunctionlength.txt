<emu-clause id="sec-setfunctionlength" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        SetFunctionLength (
          _func_: a function object,
          _length_: a non-negative integer or +&infin;,
        ): ~unused~
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
        <dt>description</dt>
        <dd>It adds a *"length"* property to _func_.</dd>
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Assert: _func_ is an extensible object that does not have a *"length"* own property.
        1. Perform ! DefinePropertyOrThrow(_func_, *"length"*, PropertyDescriptor { [[Value]]: 𝔽(_length_), [[Writable]]: *false*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *true* }).
        1. Return ~unused~.
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
