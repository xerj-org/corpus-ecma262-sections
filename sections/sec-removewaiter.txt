ECMA-262 > 25 Structured Data > 25.4 The Atomics Object > 25.4.3 Abstract Operations for Atomics > 25.4.3.9 RemoveWaiter ( _waiterList_: a WaiterList Record, _waiterRecord_: a Waiter Record, ): ~unused~ [id: sec-removewaiter]

<emu-clause id="sec-removewaiter" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          RemoveWaiter (
            _waiterList_: a WaiterList Record,
            _waiterRecord_: a Waiter Record,
          ): ~unused~
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Assert: The surrounding agent is in the critical section for _waiterList_.
          1. Assert: _waiterList_.[[Waiters]] contains _waiterRecord_.
          1. Remove _waiterRecord_ from _waiterList_.[[Waiters]].
          1. Return ~unused~.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
