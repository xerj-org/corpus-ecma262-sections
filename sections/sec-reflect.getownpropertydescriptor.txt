<emu-clause id="sec-reflect.getownpropertydescriptor" type="built-in function">
      <h1>Reflect.getOwnPropertyDescriptor ( _target_, _key_ )</h1>
      <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
      <emu-alg>
        1. If _target_ is not an Object, throw a *TypeError* exception.
        1. Let _propertyKey_ be ? ToPropertyKey(_key_).
        1. Let _propertyDesc_ be ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_target_.[[GetOwnProperty]](_propertyKey_)</emu-meta>.
        1. Return FromPropertyDescriptor(_propertyDesc_).
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
