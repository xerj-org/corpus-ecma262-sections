ECMA-262 > 23 Indexed Collections > 23.3 Uint8Array Objects > 23.3.3 Abstract Operations for Uint8Array Objects > 23.3.3.4 SkipAsciiWhitespace ( _string_: a String, _index_: a non-negative integer, ): a non-negative integer [id: sec-skipasciiwhitespace]

<emu-clause id="sec-skipasciiwhitespace" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          SkipAsciiWhitespace (
            _string_: a String,
            _index_: a non-negative integer,
          ): a non-negative integer
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _length_ be the length of _string_.
          1. Repeat, while _index_ &lt; _length_,
            1. Let _char_ be the code unit at index _index_ within _string_.
            1. If _char_ is not one of 0x0009 (TAB), 0x000A (LF), 0x000C (FF), 0x000D (CR), or 0x0020 (SPACE), then
              1. Return _index_.
            1. Set _index_ to _index_ + 1.
          1. Return _index_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
