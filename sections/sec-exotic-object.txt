<emu-clause id="sec-exotic-object">
      <h1>exotic object</h1>
      <p>object that does not have the default behaviour for one or more of the essential internal methods</p>
      <emu-note>
        <p>Any object that is not an ordinary object is an exotic object.</p>
      </emu-note>
    </emu-clause>
