        <h1>Proxy ( _target_, _handler_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. If NewTarget is *undefined*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. Return ? ProxyCreate(_target_, _handler_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
