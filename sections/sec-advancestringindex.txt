ECMA-262 > 22 Text Processing > 22.2 RegExp (Regular Expression) Objects > 22.2.7 Abstract Operations for RegExp Matching > 22.2.7.3 AdvanceStringIndex ( _string_: a String, _index_: a non-negative integer, _unicode_: a Boolean, ): a non-negative integer [id: sec-advancestringindex]

<emu-clause id="sec-advancestringindex" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          AdvanceStringIndex (
            _string_: a String,
            _index_: a non-negative integer,
            _unicode_: a Boolean,
          ): a non-negative integer
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Assert: _index_ ≤ 2<sup>53</sup> - 1.
          1. If _unicode_ is *false*, return _index_ + 1.
          1. Let _length_ be the length of _string_.
          1. If _index_ + 1 ≥ _length_, return _index_ + 1.
          1. Let _codePoint_ be CodePointAt(_string_, _index_).
          1. Return _index_ + _codePoint_.[[CodeUnitCount]].
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
