<emu-clause id="sec-weakset.prototype.add" type="built-in function">
        <h1>WeakSet.prototype.add ( _value_ )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _weakSet_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_weakSet_, [[WeakSetData]]).
          1. If CanBeHeldWeakly(_value_) is *false*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. For each element _entry_ of _weakSet_.[[WeakSetData]], do
            1. If _entry_ is not ~empty~ and SameValue(_entry_, _value_) is *true*, then
              1. Return _weakSet_.
          1. Append _value_ to _weakSet_.[[WeakSetData]].
          1. Return _weakSet_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
