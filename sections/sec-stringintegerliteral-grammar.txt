ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.1 Type Conversion > 7.1.16 StringToBigInt ( _string_: a String, ): a BigInt or *undefined* > 7.1.16.1 StringIntegerLiteral Grammar [id: sec-stringintegerliteral-grammar]

<emu-clause id="sec-stringintegerliteral-grammar">
        <h1>StringIntegerLiteral Grammar</h1>
        <p>StringToBigInt uses the following grammar.</p>
        <h2>Syntax</h2>
        <emu-grammar type="definition">
          StringIntegerLiteral :::
            StrWhiteSpace?
            StrWhiteSpace? StrIntegerLiteral StrWhiteSpace?

          StrIntegerLiteral :::
            SignedInteger[~Sep]
            NonDecimalIntegerLiteral[~Sep]
        </emu-grammar>
      </emu-clause>
