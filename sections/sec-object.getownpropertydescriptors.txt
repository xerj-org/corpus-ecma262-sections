<emu-clause id="sec-object.getownpropertydescriptors" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptors ( _obj_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _coerced_ be ? ToObject(_obj_).
          1. Let _ownKeys_ be ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_coerced_.[[OwnPropertyKeys]]()</emu-meta>.
          1. Let _descs_ be OrdinaryObjectCreate(%Object.prototype%).
          1. For each element _key_ of _ownKeys_, do
            1. Let _propertyDesc_ be ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_coerced_.[[GetOwnProperty]](_key_)</emu-meta>.
            1. Let _propertyDescObj_ be FromPropertyDescriptor(_propertyDesc_).
            1. If _propertyDescObj_ is not *undefined*, perform ! CreateDataPropertyOrThrow(_descs_, _key_, _propertyDescObj_).
          1. Return _descs_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
