ECMA-262 > 25 Structured Data > 25.2 SharedArrayBuffer Objects > 25.2.3 The SharedArrayBuffer Constructor > 25.2.3.1 SharedArrayBuffer ( _length_ [ , _options_ ] ) [id: sec-sharedarraybuffer-length]

<emu-clause id="sec-sharedarraybuffer-length" type="built-in function">
        <h1>SharedArrayBuffer ( _length_ [ , _options_ ] )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. If NewTarget is *undefined*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. Let _byteLength_ be ? ToIndex(_length_).
          1. Let _requestedMaxByteLength_ be ? GetArrayBufferMaxByteLengthOption(_options_).
          1. Return ? AllocateSharedArrayBuffer(NewTarget, _byteLength_, _requestedMaxByteLength_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
