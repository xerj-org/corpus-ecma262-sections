ECMA-262 > 27 Control Abstraction Objects > 27.1 Iteration > 27.1.5 Async-from-Sync Iterator Objects > 27.1.5.2 The %AsyncFromSyncIteratorPrototype% Object > 27.1.5.2.1 %AsyncFromSyncIteratorPrototype%.next ( [ _value_ ] ) [id: sec-%asyncfromsynciteratorprototype%.next]

<emu-clause id="sec-%asyncfromsynciteratorprototype%.next" type="built-in function">
          <h1>%AsyncFromSyncIteratorPrototype%.next ( [ _value_ ] )</h1>
          <emu-alg>
            1. Let _obj_ be the *this* value.
            1. Assert: _obj_ is an Object that has a [[SyncIteratorRecord]] internal slot.
            1. Let _promiseCapability_ be ! NewPromiseCapability(%Promise%).
            1. Let _syncIteratorRecord_ be _obj_.[[SyncIteratorRecord]].
            1. If _value_ is present, then
              1. Let _result_ be Completion(IteratorNext(_syncIteratorRecord_, _value_)).
            1. Else,
              1. Let _result_ be Completion(IteratorNext(_syncIteratorRecord_)).
            1. IfAbruptRejectPromise(_result_, _promiseCapability_).
            1. Return AsyncFromSyncIteratorContinuation(_result_, _promiseCapability_, _syncIteratorRecord_, *true*).
          </emu-alg>
        </emu-clause>
