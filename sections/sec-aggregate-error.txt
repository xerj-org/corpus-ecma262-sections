ECMA-262 > 20 Fundamental Objects > 20.5 Error Objects > 20.5.7 AggregateError Objects > 20.5.7.1 The AggregateError Constructor > 20.5.7.1.1 AggregateError ( _errors_, _message_ [ , _options_ ] ) [id: sec-aggregate-error]

<emu-clause id="sec-aggregate-error" type="built-in function">
          <h1>AggregateError ( _errors_, _message_ [ , _options_ ] )</h1>
          <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
          <emu-alg>
            1. If NewTarget is *undefined*, let _newTarget_ be the active function object; else let _newTarget_ be NewTarget.
            1. Let _obj_ be ? OrdinaryCreateFromConstructor(_newTarget_, *"%AggregateError.prototype%"*, « [[ErrorData]] »).
            1. If _message_ is not *undefined*, then
              1. Let _messageString_ be ? ToString(_message_).
              1. Perform CreateNonEnumerableDataPropertyOrThrow(_obj_, *"message"*, _messageString_).
            1. Perform ? InstallErrorCause(_obj_, _options_).
            1. Let _errorsList_ be ? IteratorToList(? GetIterator(_errors_, ~sync~)).
            1. Perform ! DefinePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, *"errors"*, PropertyDescriptor { [[Configurable]]: *true*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Writable]]: *true*, [[Value]]: CreateArrayFromList(_errorsList_) }).
            1. Return _obj_.
          </emu-alg>
        </emu-clause>
