<emu-clause id="sec-atomics.exchange" type="built-in function">
      <h1>Atomics.exchange ( _ta_, _index_, _value_ )</h1>
      <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _second_ be a new read-modify-write modification function with parameters (_oldBytes_, _newBytes_) that captures nothing and performs the following steps atomically when called:
          1. Return _newBytes_.
        1. Return ? AtomicReadModifyWrite(_ta_, _index_, _value_, _second_).
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
