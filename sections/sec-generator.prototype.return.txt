<emu-clause id="sec-generator.prototype.return" type="built-in function">
        <h1>%GeneratorPrototype%.return ( _value_ )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _gen_ be the *this* value.
          1. Let _completion_ be ReturnCompletion(_value_).
          1. Return ? GeneratorResumeAbrupt(_gen_, _completion_, ~empty~).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
