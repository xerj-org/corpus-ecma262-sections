<emu-clause id="sec-array.prototype.join" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Array.prototype.join ( _separator_ )</h1>
        <p>This method converts the elements of the array to Strings, and then concatenates these Strings, separated by occurrences of the _separator_. If no separator is provided, a single comma is used as the separator.</p>
        <p>It performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be ? ToObject(*this* value).
          1. Let _length_ be ? LengthOfArrayLike(_obj_).
          1. If _separator_ is *undefined*, let _separatorString_ be *","*.
          1. Else, let _separatorString_ be ? ToString(_separator_).
          1. Let _result_ be the empty String.
          1. Let _k_ be 0.
          1. Repeat, while _k_ &lt; _length_,
            1. If _k_ > 0, set _result_ to the string-concatenation of _result_ and _separatorString_.
            1. Let _element_ be ? Get(_obj_, ! ToString(𝔽(_k_))).
            1. If _element_ is neither *undefined* nor *null*, then
              1. Let _elementString_ be ? ToString(_element_).
              1. Set _result_ to the string-concatenation of _result_ and _elementString_.
            1. Set _k_ to _k_ + 1.
          1. Return _result_.
        </emu-alg>
        <emu-note>
          <p>This method is intentionally generic; it does not require that its *this* value be an Array. Therefore, it can be transferred to other kinds of objects for use as a method.</p>
        </emu-note>
      </emu-clause>
