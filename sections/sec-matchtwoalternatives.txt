<emu-clause id="sec-matchtwoalternatives" type="abstract operation">
          <h1>
            MatchTwoAlternatives (
              _m1_: a Matcher,
              _m2_: a Matcher,
            ): a Matcher
          </h1>
          <dl class="header">
          </dl>
          <emu-alg>
            1. Return a new Matcher with parameters (_matchState_, _continue_) that captures _m1_ and _m2_ and performs the following steps when called:
              1. Assert: _matchState_ is a MatchState.
              1. Assert: _continue_ is a MatcherContinuation.
              1. Let _result_ be _m1_(_matchState_, _continue_).
              1. If _result_ is not ~failure~, return _result_.
              1. Return _m2_(_matchState_, _continue_).
          </emu-alg>
        </emu-clause>
