<emu-clause id="sec-symbol.keyfor" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Symbol.keyFor ( _symbol_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. If _symbol_ is not a Symbol, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. Return KeyForSymbol(_symbol_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
