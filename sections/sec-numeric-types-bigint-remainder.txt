<emu-clause id="sec-numeric-types-bigint-remainder" type="numeric method">
          <h1>
            BigInt::remainder (
              _numerator_: a BigInt,
              _denominator_: a BigInt,
            ): either a normal completion containing a BigInt or a throw completion
          </h1>
          <dl class="header">
          </dl>
          <emu-alg>
            1. If _denominator_ = *0*<sub>ℤ</sub>, throw a *RangeError* exception.
            1. If _numerator_ = *0*<sub>ℤ</sub>, return *0*<sub>ℤ</sub>.
            1. Let _quotient_ be ℝ(_numerator_) / ℝ(_denominator_).
            1. Let _truncatedQuotient_ be ℤ(truncate(_quotient_)).
            1. Return _numerator_ - (_denominator_ × _truncatedQuotient_).
          </emu-alg>
          <emu-note>The sign of the result is the sign of the dividend.</emu-note>
        </emu-clause>
