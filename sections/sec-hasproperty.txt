ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.3 Operations on Objects > 7.3.11 HasProperty ( _obj_: an Object, _propertyKey_: a property key, ): either a normal completion containing a Boolean or a throw completion [id: sec-hasproperty]

<emu-clause id="sec-hasproperty" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        HasProperty (
          _obj_: an Object,
          _propertyKey_: a property key,
        ): either a normal completion containing a Boolean or a throw completion
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
        <dt>description</dt>
        <dd>It is used to determine whether an object has a property with the specified property key. The property may be either own or inherited.</dd>
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Return ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_obj_.[[HasProperty]](_propertyKey_)</emu-meta>.
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
