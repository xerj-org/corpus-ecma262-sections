<emu-clause id="sec-asyncdisposablestack.prototype.disposeAsync" type="built-in function">
        <h1>AsyncDisposableStack.prototype.disposeAsync ( )</h1>
        <p>This async method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _asyncDisposableStack_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_asyncDisposableStack_, [[AsyncDisposableState]]).
          1. If _asyncDisposableStack_.[[AsyncDisposableState]] is ~disposed~, return *undefined*.
          1. Set _asyncDisposableStack_.[[AsyncDisposableState]] to ~disposed~.
          1. Return ? DisposeResources(_asyncDisposableStack_.[[DisposableResourceStack]], NormalCompletion(*undefined*)).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
