<emu-clause id="sec-date.prototype.settime" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Date.prototype.setTime ( _time_ )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _dateObj_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_dateObj_, [[DateValue]]).
          1. Let _t_ be ? ToNumber(_time_).
          1. Let _v_ be TimeClip(_t_).
          1. Set _dateObj_.[[DateValue]] to _v_.
          1. Return _v_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
