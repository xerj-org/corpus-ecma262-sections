<emu-clause id="sec-dataview.prototype.setint16" type="built-in function">
        <h1>DataView.prototype.setInt16 ( _byteOffset_, _value_ [ , _littleEndian_ ] )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _view_ be the *this* value.
          1. If _littleEndian_ is not present, set _littleEndian_ to *false*.
          1. Perform ? SetViewValue(_view_, _byteOffset_, _littleEndian_, ~int16~, _value_).
          1. Return *undefined*.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
