<emu-clause id="sec-%typedarray%.prototype.toreversed" type="built-in function">
        <h1>%TypedArray%.prototype.toReversed ( )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be the *this* value.
          1. Let _taRecord_ be ? ValidateTypedArray(_obj_, ~seq-cst~).
          1. Let _length_ be TypedArrayLength(_taRecord_).
          1. Let _resultArray_ be ? TypedArrayCreateSameType(_obj_, _length_).
          1. Let _k_ be 0.
          1. Repeat, while _k_ &lt; _length_,
            1. Let _from_ be _length_ - _k_ - 1.
            1. Let _fromValue_ be TypedArrayGetElement(_obj_, 𝔽(_from_)).
            1. Perform ! TypedArraySetElement(_resultArray_, 𝔽(_k_), _fromValue_).
            1. Set _k_ to _k_ + 1.
          1. Return _resultArray_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
