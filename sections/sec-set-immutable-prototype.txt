        <h1>
          SetImmutablePrototype (
            _obj_: an Object,
            _proto_: an Object or *null*,
          ): either a normal completion containing a Boolean or a throw completion
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _current_ be ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_obj_.[[GetPrototypeOf]]()</emu-meta>.
