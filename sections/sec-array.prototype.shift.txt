<emu-clause id="sec-array.prototype.shift" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Array.prototype.shift ( )</h1>
        <p>This method removes the first element of the array and returns it.</p>
        <p>It performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be ? ToObject(*this* value).
          1. Let _length_ be ? LengthOfArrayLike(_obj_).
          1. If _length_ = 0, then
            1. Perform ? Set(_obj_, *"length"*, *+0*<sub>𝔽</sub>, *true*).
            1. Return *undefined*.
          1. Let _first_ be ? Get(_obj_, *"0"*).
          1. Let _k_ be 1.
          1. Repeat, while _k_ &lt; _length_,
            1. Let _from_ be ! ToString(𝔽(_k_)).
            1. Let _to_ be ! ToString(𝔽(_k_ - 1)).
            1. Let _fromPresent_ be ? HasProperty(_obj_, _from_).
            1. If _fromPresent_ is *true*, then
              1. Let _fromValue_ be ? Get(_obj_, _from_).
              1. Perform ? Set(_obj_, _to_, _fromValue_, *true*).
            1. Else,
              1. Assert: _fromPresent_ is *false*.
              1. Perform ? DeletePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, _to_).
            1. Set _k_ to _k_ + 1.
          1. Perform ? DeletePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, ! ToString(𝔽(_length_ - 1))).
          1. Perform ? Set(_obj_, *"length"*, 𝔽(_length_ - 1), *true*).
          1. Return _first_.
        </emu-alg>
        <emu-note>
          <p>This method is intentionally generic; it does not require that its *this* value be an Array. Therefore it can be transferred to other kinds of objects for use as a method.</p>
        </emu-note>
      </emu-clause>
