<emu-clause id="sec-array.prototype.flat" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Array.prototype.flat ( [ _depth_ ] )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be ? ToObject(*this* value).
          1. Let _sourceLength_ be ? LengthOfArrayLike(_obj_).
          1. Let _depthNumber_ be 1.
          1. If _depth_ is not *undefined*, then
            1. Set _depthNumber_ to ? ToIntegerOrInfinity(_depth_).
            1. If _depthNumber_ &lt; 0, set _depthNumber_ to 0.
          1. Let _array_ be ? ArraySpeciesCreate(_obj_, 0).
          1. Perform ? FlattenIntoArray(_array_, _obj_, _sourceLength_, 0, _depthNumber_).
          1. Return _array_.
        </emu-alg>
