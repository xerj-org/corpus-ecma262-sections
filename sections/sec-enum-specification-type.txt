<emu-clause id="sec-enum-specification-type">
      <h1>The Enum Specification Type</h1>
      <p><dfn variants="enum,enums">Enums</dfn> are values which are internal to the specification and not directly observable from ECMAScript code. Enums are denoted in upper kebab case using a ~sans-serif~ typeface. For instance, a Completion Record's [[Type]] field takes on values like ~normal~, ~return~, or ~throw~. Enums have no characteristics other than their name. The name of an enum serves no purpose other than to distinguish it from other enums, and implies nothing about its usage or meaning in context.</p>
    </emu-clause>
