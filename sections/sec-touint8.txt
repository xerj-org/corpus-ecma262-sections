ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.1 Type Conversion > 7.1.13 ToUint8 ( _arg_: an ECMAScript language value, ): either a normal completion containing an integral Number or a throw completion [id: sec-touint8]

<emu-clause id="sec-touint8" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        ToUint8 (
          _arg_: an ECMAScript language value,
        ): either a normal completion containing an integral Number or a throw completion
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
        <dt>description</dt>
        <dd>It converts _arg_ to one of 2<sup>8</sup> integral Number values in the inclusive interval from *+0*<sub>𝔽</sub> to *255*<sub>𝔽</sub>.</dd>
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _int_ be ? ToIntegerOrInfinity(_arg_).
        1. Return 𝔽(ToFixedSizeInteger(_int_, ~unsigned~, 8)).
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
