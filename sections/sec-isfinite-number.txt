<emu-clause id="sec-isfinite-number" type="built-in function">
      <h1>isFinite ( _value_ )</h1>
      <p>This function is the <dfn>%isFinite%</dfn> intrinsic object.</p>
      <p>It performs the following steps when called:</p>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _number_ be ? ToNumber(_value_).
        1. If _number_ is a finite Number, return *true*.
        1. Return *false*.
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
