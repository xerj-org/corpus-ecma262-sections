<emu-clause id="sec-method">
      <h1>method</h1>
      <p>function that is the value of a property</p>
      <emu-note>
        <p>When a function is called as a method of an object, the object is passed to the function as its *this* value.</p>
      </emu-note>
    </emu-clause>
