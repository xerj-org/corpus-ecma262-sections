<emu-clause id="sec-global-environment-records-createimmutablebinding-n-s" type="concrete method">
          <h1>
            CreateImmutableBinding (
              _name_: a String,
              _strict_: a Boolean,
            ): either a normal completion containing ~unused~ or a throw completion
          </h1>
          <dl class="header">
            <dt>for</dt>
            <dd>a Global Environment Record _envRecord_</dd>

            <dt>description</dt>
            <dd>It creates a new immutable binding for the name _name_ that is uninitialized. A binding must not already exist in this Environment Record for _name_. If _strict_ is *true*, the new binding is marked as a strict binding.</dd>
          </dl>
          <emu-alg>
            1. Let _declRecord_ be _envRecord_.[[DeclarativeRecord]].
            1. If ! _declRecord_.HasBinding(_name_) is *true*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
            1. Return ! _declRecord_.CreateImmutableBinding(_name_, _strict_).
          </emu-alg>
        </emu-clause>
