<emu-clause id="sec-AllImportAttributesSupported" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          AllImportAttributesSupported (
            _attrs_: a List of ImportAttribute Records,
          ): a Boolean
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
          <dt>description</dt>
          <dd></dd>
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _supported_ be HostGetSupportedImportAttributes().
          1. For each ImportAttribute Record _attr_ of _attrs_, do
            1. If _supported_ does not contain _attr_.[[Key]], return *false*.
          1. Return *true*.
        </emu-alg>

        
      </emu-clause>
