ECMA-262 > 7 Abstract Operations > 7.1 Type Conversion > 7.1.16 StringToBigInt ( _string_: a String, ): a BigInt or *undefined* [id: sec-stringtobigint]

<emu-clause id="sec-stringtobigint" type="abstract operation">
      <h1>
        StringToBigInt (
          _string_: a String,
        ): a BigInt or *undefined*
      </h1>
      <dl class="header">
      </dl>
      <emu-alg>
        1. Let _literal_ be ParseText(_string_, |StringIntegerLiteral|).
        1. If _literal_ is a List of errors, return *undefined*.
        1. Let _mv_ be the MV of _literal_.
        1. Assert: _mv_ is an integer.
        1. Return ℤ(_mv_).
      </emu-alg>

      

      
    </emu-clause>
