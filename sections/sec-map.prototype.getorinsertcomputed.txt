<emu-clause id="sec-map.prototype.getorinsertcomputed" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Map.prototype.getOrInsertComputed ( _key_, _callback_ )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _map_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_map_, [[MapData]]).
          1. If IsCallable(_callback_) is *false*, throw a *TypeError* exception.
          1. Set _key_ to CanonicalizeKeyedCollectionKey(_key_).
          1. For each Record { [[Key]], [[Value]] } _entry_ of _map_.[[MapData]], do
            1. If _entry_.[[Key]] is not ~empty~ and SameValue(_entry_.[[Key]], _key_) is *true*, return _entry_.[[Value]].
          1. Let _value_ be ? Call(_callback_, *undefined*, « _key_ »).
          1. NOTE: The Map may have been modified during execution of _callback_.
          1. For each Record { [[Key]], [[Value]] } _entry_ of _map_.[[MapData]], do
            1. If _entry_.[[Key]] is not ~empty~ and SameValue(_entry_.[[Key]], _key_) is *true*, then
              1. Set _entry_.[[Value]] to _value_.
              1. Return _value_.
          1. Let _entry_ be the Record { [[Key]]: _key_, [[Value]]: _value_ }.
          1. Append _entry_ to _map_.[[MapData]].
          1. Return _value_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
