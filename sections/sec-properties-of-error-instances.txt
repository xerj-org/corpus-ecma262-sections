<emu-clause id="sec-properties-of-error-instances">
      <h1>Properties of Error Instances</h1>
      <p>Error instances are ordinary objects that inherit properties from the Error prototype object and have an [[ErrorData]] internal slot whose value is *undefined*. The only specified use of [[ErrorData]] is to identify Error, AggregateError, SuppressedError, and _NativeError_ instances as Error objects within `Object.prototype.toString` and `Error.isError`.</p>
    </emu-clause>
