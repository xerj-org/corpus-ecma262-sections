<emu-clause id="sec-date.prototype.gethours" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Date.prototype.getHours ( )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _dateObj_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_dateObj_, [[DateValue]]).
          1. Let _tv_ be _dateObj_.[[DateValue]].
          1. If _tv_ is *NaN*, return *NaN*.
          1. Return 𝔽(HourFromTime(LocalTime(_tv_))).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
