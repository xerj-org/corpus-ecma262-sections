ECMA-262 > 21 Numbers and Dates > 21.4 Date Objects > 21.4.1 Overview of Date Objects and Definitions of Abstract Operations > 21.4.1.29 MakeDate ( _day_: a Number, _time_: a Number, ): a finite Number or *NaN* [id: sec-makedate]

<emu-clause id="sec-makedate" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          MakeDate (
            _day_: a Number,
            _time_: a Number,
          ): a finite Number or *NaN*
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
          <dt>description</dt>
          <dd>It calculates a number of milliseconds.</dd>
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. If _day_ is not a finite Number or _time_ is not a finite Number, return *NaN*.
          1. Let _tv_ be _day_ × 𝔽(MillisecondsPerDay) + _time_.
          1. If _tv_ is not a finite Number, return *NaN*.
          1. Return _tv_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
