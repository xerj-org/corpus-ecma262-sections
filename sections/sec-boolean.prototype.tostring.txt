<emu-clause id="sec-boolean.prototype.tostring" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Boolean.prototype.toString ( )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _bool_ be ? ThisBooleanValue(*this* value).
          1. If _bool_ is *true*, return *"true"*.
          1. Return *"false"*.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
