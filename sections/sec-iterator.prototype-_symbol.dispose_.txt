<emu-clause id="sec-iterator.prototype-%symbol.dispose%" type="built-in function">
          <h1>Iterator.prototype [ %Symbol.dispose% ] ( )</h1>
          <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
          <emu-alg>
            1. Let _obj_ be the *this* value.
            1. Let _return_ be ? GetMethod(_obj_, *"return"*).
            1. If _return_ is not *undefined*, then
              1. Perform ? Call(_return_, _obj_).
            1. Return *undefined*.
          </emu-alg>
          <p>The value of the *"name"* property of this method is *"[Symbol.dispose]"*.</p>
        </emu-clause>
