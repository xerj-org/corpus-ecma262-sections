<emu-clause id="sec-createunmappedargumentsobject" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          CreateUnmappedArgumentsObject (
            _argList_: a List of ECMAScript language values,
          ): an ordinary object
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _length_ be the number of elements in _argList_.
          1. Let _obj_ be OrdinaryObjectCreate(%Object.prototype%, « [[ParameterMap]] »).
          1. Set _obj_.[[ParameterMap]] to *undefined*.
          1. Perform ! DefinePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, *"length"*, PropertyDescriptor { [[Value]]: 𝔽(_length_), [[Writable]]: *true*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *true* }).
          1. Let _index_ be 0.
          1. Repeat, while _index_ &lt; _length_,
            1. Let _value_ be _argList_[_index_].
            1. Perform ! CreateDataPropertyOrThrow(_obj_, ! ToString(𝔽(_index_)), _value_).
            1. Set _index_ to _index_ + 1.
          1. Perform ! DefinePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, %Symbol.iterator%, PropertyDescriptor { [[Value]]: %Array.prototype.values%, [[Writable]]: *true*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *true* }).
          1. Perform ! DefinePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, *"callee"*, PropertyDescriptor { [[Getter]]: %ThrowTypeError%, [[Setter]]: %ThrowTypeError%, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *false* }).
          1. Return _obj_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
