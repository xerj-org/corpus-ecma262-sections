<emu-clause id="sec-array.prototype.pop" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Array.prototype.pop ( )</h1>
        <emu-note>
          <p>This method removes the last element of the array and returns it.</p>
        </emu-note>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be ? ToObject(*this* value).
          1. Let _length_ be ? LengthOfArrayLike(_obj_).
          1. If _length_ = 0, then
            1. Perform ? Set(_obj_, *"length"*, *+0*<sub>𝔽</sub>, *true*).
            1. Return *undefined*.
          1. Assert: _length_ > 0.
          1. Let _newLength_ be 𝔽(_length_ - 1).
          1. Let _index_ be ! ToString(_newLength_).
          1. Let _element_ be ? Get(_obj_, _index_).
          1. Perform ? DeletePropertyOrThrow(_obj_, _index_).
          1. Perform ? Set(_obj_, *"length"*, _newLength_, *true*).
          1. Return _element_.
        </emu-alg>
        <emu-note>
          <p>This method is intentionally generic; it does not require that its *this* value be an Array. Therefore it can be transferred to other kinds of objects for use as a method.</p>
        </emu-note>
      </emu-clause>
