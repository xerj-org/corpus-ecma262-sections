<emu-clause id="sec-date.prototype.totimestring" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Date.prototype.toTimeString ( )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _dateObj_ be the *this* value.
          1. Perform ? RequireInternalSlot(_dateObj_, [[DateValue]]).
          1. Let _tv_ be _dateObj_.[[DateValue]].
          1. If _tv_ is *NaN*, return *"Invalid Date"*.
          1. Let _localTime_ be LocalTime(_tv_).
          1. Return the string-concatenation of TimeString(_localTime_) and TimeZoneString(_tv_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
