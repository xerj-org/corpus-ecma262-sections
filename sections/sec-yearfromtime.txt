        <h1>
          YearFromTime (
            _tv_: a finite time value,
          ): an integer
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
          <dt>description</dt>
          <dd>It returns the year in which _tv_ falls.</dd>
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. [declared="y"] Return the largest integer _y_ (closest to +∞) such that TimeFromYear(_y_) ≤ _tv_.
        </emu-alg>
