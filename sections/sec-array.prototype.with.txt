<emu-clause id="sec-array.prototype.with" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Array.prototype.with ( _index_, _value_ )</h1>
        <p>This method performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _obj_ be ? ToObject(*this* value).
          1. Let _length_ be ? LengthOfArrayLike(_obj_).
          1. Let _actualIndex_ be ? ToAbsoluteIndex(_index_, _length_).
          1. If _actualIndex_ &lt; 0 or _actualIndex_ ≥ _length_, throw a *RangeError* exception.
          1. Let _array_ be ? ArrayCreate(_length_).
          1. Let _k_ be 0.
          1. Repeat, while _k_ &lt; _length_,
            1. Let _propertyKey_ be ! ToString(𝔽(_k_)).
            1. If _k_ = _actualIndex_, let _fromValue_ be _value_.
            1. Else, let _fromValue_ be ? Get(_obj_, _propertyKey_).
            1. Perform ! CreateDataPropertyOrThrow(_array_, _propertyKey_, _fromValue_).
            1. Set _k_ to _k_ + 1.
          1. Return _array_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
