<emu-clause id="sec-object.hasown" type="built-in function">
        <h1>Object.hasOwn ( _obj_, _key_ )</h1>
        <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Let _coerced_ be ? ToObject(_obj_).
          1. Let _propertyKey_ be ? ToPropertyKey(_key_).
          1. Return ? HasOwnProperty(_coerced_, _propertyKey_).
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
