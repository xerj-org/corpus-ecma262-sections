        <h1>WeakRef.prototype</h1>
        <p>The initial value of `WeakRef.prototype` is the WeakRef prototype object.</p>
        <p>This property has the attributes { [[Writable]]: *false*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *false* }.</p>
      </emu-clause>
