<emu-clause id="sec-removewaiters" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          RemoveWaiters (
            _waiterList_: a WaiterList Record,
            _count_: a non-negative integer or +&infin;,
          ): a List of Waiter Records
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Assert: The surrounding agent is in the critical section for _waiterList_.
          1. Let _length_ be the number of elements in _waiterList_.[[Waiters]].
          1. Set _count_ to min(_count_, _length_).
          1. Let _waiters_ be a List whose elements are the first _count_ elements of _waiterList_.[[Waiters]].
          1. Remove the first _count_ elements of _waiterList_.[[Waiters]].
          1. Return _waiters_.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
