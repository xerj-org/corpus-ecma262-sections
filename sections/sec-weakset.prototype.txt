        <h1>WeakSet.prototype</h1>
        <p>The initial value of `WeakSet.prototype` is the WeakSet prototype object.</p>
        <p>This property has the attributes { [[Writable]]: *false*, [[Enumerable]]: *false*, [[Configurable]]: *false* }.</p>
