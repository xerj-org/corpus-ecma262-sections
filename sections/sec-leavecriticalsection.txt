ECMA-262 > 25 Structured Data > 25.4 The Atomics Object > 25.4.3 Abstract Operations for Atomics > 25.4.3.7 LeaveCriticalSection ( _waiterList_: a WaiterList Record, ): ~unused~ [id: sec-leavecriticalsection]

<emu-clause id="sec-leavecriticalsection" type="abstract operation">
        <h1>
          LeaveCriticalSection (
            _waiterList_: a WaiterList Record,
          ): ~unused~
        </h1>
        <dl class="header">
        </dl>
        <emu-alg>
          1. Assert: The surrounding agent is in the critical section for _waiterList_.
          1. Let _agentRecord_ be the Agent Record of the surrounding agent.
          1. Let _execution_ be _agentRecord_.[[CandidateExecution]].
          1. Let _eventsRecord_ be the Agent Events Record of _execution_.[[EventsRecords]] whose [[AgentSignifier]] is AgentSignifier().
          1. Let _leaveEvent_ be a new Synchronize event.
          1. Append _leaveEvent_ to _eventsRecord_.[[EventList]].
          1. Set _waiterList_.[[MostRecentLeaveEvent]] to _leaveEvent_.
          1. Leave the critical section for _waiterList_.
          1. Return ~unused~.
        </emu-alg>
      </emu-clause>
