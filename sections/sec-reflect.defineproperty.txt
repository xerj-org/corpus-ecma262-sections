<emu-clause id="sec-reflect.defineproperty" type="built-in function">
      <h1>Reflect.defineProperty ( _target_, _key_, _attrs_ )</h1>
      <p>This function performs the following steps when called:</p>
      <emu-alg>
        1. If _target_ is not an Object, throw a *TypeError* exception.
        1. Let _propertyKey_ be ? ToPropertyKey(_key_).
        1. Let _propertyDesc_ be ? ToPropertyDescriptor(_attrs_).
        1. Return ? <emu-meta effects="user-code">_target_.[[DefineOwnProperty]](_propertyKey_, _propertyDesc_)</emu-meta>.
      </emu-alg>
    </emu-clause>
